What it does

Three jobs you stop doing by hand.

01

Queue once. Post all week.

Scheduled, recurring and random-pick posts for every account, from one screen. Bring your posts in from a spreadsheet.

02

Follow up when a post takes off.

Set a like or view count. When a post passes it, XToolsPro replies under it with your link while people are still looking.

03

Replies, likes and follows on your rules.

Keyword-based likes and follows, AI-drafted replies, and DM auto-replies. You set the pace and it keeps to it.

How it works

It runs on your PC, in real Chrome windows.

  1. Install it on your Windows PC

    Windows 10 or later. 8 GB of memory recommended.

  2. Log in to your X accounts

    Each account gets its own Chrome window. No developer account and no API keys.

  3. Set the queue and the rules

    It works while the PC is on. Read the log when you get home.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need X API keys?

No. XToolsPro works through Chrome on your PC, so there is no developer account and no API bill.

How many accounts can it run?

The Japanese edition holds about 100 accounts on one PC. Around 10 can post at the same moment, so stagger the times if you run more.

When does the English version launch?

There is no date yet. If enough people join this list, we build English support for the most requested job first and email you.

Does it work on Mac?

No. It needs Windows 10 or later.

Who makes it?

One developer in Japan, working as Youpapa. He builds, sells and supports the Japanese edition himself.
